Questions

Do Pro subscribers see my banner?

Yes. Pro removes Google display ads, but direct sponsor banners show to every visitor by default.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, a headline, an optional short line, the button text, the link, and your brand colors. We put the banner together and send you a preview before it goes live.

Can I see it before I commit?

Yes. Open any page with the sample banner, and before your run starts we'll send a preview with your own creative.

Will my business affect the conditions call?

No. Verdicts come only from NOAA wind, wave, storm and advisory data, and the banner is kept separate from them and labeled as an ad.
