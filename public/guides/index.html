Boating guides

The Great Lakes make their own weather, and every coast has its own traps: inlets on an outgoing tide, cold fronts, shallow bays that chop up fast. A wind that's harmless off one shore is dangerous off another. These plain-English guides explain how to read the conditions — and the numbers on every shouldiboat.com spot page — so you can make a good call before you tow the boat.
