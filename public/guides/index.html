Great Lakes boating guides

The Great Lakes make their own weather. Short, steep chop builds fast, the water runs cold most of the year, and a wind that's harmless off one shore is dangerous off another. These plain-English guides explain how to read the conditions — and the numbers on every shouldiboat.com spot page — so you can make a good call before you tow the boat.

These guides are general education, not official forecasts or navigation advice. Conditions on the water change fast — always check the official NWS marine forecasts, file a float plan, wear your life jacket, and use your own judgment.
