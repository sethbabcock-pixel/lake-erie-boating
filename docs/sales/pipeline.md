# Sponsorship pipeline

Owner: sales-sponsorships. Started 2026-11-06. Last updated 2026-11-06.

Rules: only real, verified businesses. Cite the source for every prospect. Never invent contacts or emails; "Source" is where we found the business and its public contact route (website contact form, published inbox). The human owner sends all outreach; this file only tracks it.

## Stage definitions

| Stage | Meaning | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect | Business verified to exist near a launch spot we cover; source recorded; no outreach yet | Owner approves and sends touch 1 |
| Contacted | Touch 1 (or later) sent by the owner | Any reply, or sequence finished with no reply (then Lost/nurture) |
| Meeting | Call or visit scheduled or held | Sponsor states launch/region and dates they want |
| Proposal | Rate and placement sent (rate only after the owner approves the rate card) | Verbal yes/no |
| Won | Signed terms (legal-compliance reviewed) and campaign entered in admin | Campaign run, report sent |
| Lost | Declined or silent after 3 touches; record the reason | Revisit next season if useful |
