Owner: sales-sponsorships. Drafted 2026-11-08. DRAFT ONLY: the owner reviews and sends every message. Nothing here has been sent.

Goal: get local boating businesses to link to their local shouldiboat.com spot page (direct visitors, plus the AdSense "established presence" signal), and build the future sponsor pipeline.

## READ FIRST: verification status (owner action needed)

This session could not open the businesses' own websites (WebFetch failed DNS on every business domain; the sandbox network allowlist blocks them). Prospects below were therefore verified only through WebSearch results (directory listings, chamber/tourism pages, booking sites). What that means:

- Existence: each business appeared in at least one third-party listing with an address or phone. Not confirmed on the business's own site.
- Website: the domain was seen in a search result or in the business's own listed email domain. Not opened.
- Contact route: emails shown below were listed publicly for the business in search results. Where I saw no email, the route says "website contact page (confirm)". I did not invent or guess any address.
- Before sending: spend 1 minute per prospect opening the site, confirming it is still operating and copying the email or form URL from the site itself. If the site shows a different route, use that one. Drop any prospect that looks closed.
- Stale-data flags: Capt. Hook's and The Tackle Box details come from a 2012 listing; Marathon Bait & Tackle and White Waters Paradise come from a directory aggregator. Check these first.
- Only business-published, role-type contacts are used. I skipped City of Biloxi staff emails (named individuals) and private club member contacts.
- Coverage gaps: no Louisiana (Grand Isle) and no Florida Atlantic mainland inlet (Ponce, Canaveral, Jupiter, Fort Lauderdale) prospects reached the bar, because searches returned only aggregator/booking pages with no website or contact route. Next batch should target those.

## Offer (same for every prospect)

1. A free "Today's boating conditions at [area]" text link for their site. It goes to a free GO / CAUTION / NO-GO page built from NOAA/NWS data. No signup.
2. (No listing on shouldiboat.com is offered yet. The site has no partners/resources page. Add this back only once one exists.)
3. No payment, no obligation, no exclusivity. Remove anytime.
4. Soft P.S. only: sponsorships exist; sponsors are always labeled and never influence a verdict.
5. Copy rule: shouldiboat.com is a planning aid, not a safety guarantee. Never say "safe". Tell people to check official marine forecasts and use their own judgment.

Note: there's no partners/resources page on shouldiboat.com, so the emails below don't promise a listing.
